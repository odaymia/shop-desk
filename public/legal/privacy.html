Privacy Policy

Genie Auto Center · San Diego, CA · Effective September 16, 2026

Genie Auto Center ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect from our service customers and how we use it, including our text-message (SMS) program.

Information we collect

When you bring your vehicle to us for service, we collect the information you provide at the counter: your name, mobile phone number, email address (if given), vehicle details, and the service performed. We use this to service your vehicle, prepare your estimate and invoice, and communicate with you about your repair.

Text messages (SMS)

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number, or your consent to receive text messages, with any third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Your number is used only by Genie Auto Center to send you service-related messages about your own vehicle — such as a secure link to review, approve, and sign your repair estimate or invoice.

Message frequency is occasional — generally one or two messages per service visit. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message, and reply HELP for help. See our SMS Terms & Conditions for full program details.

How we use your information

We use your information to provide and document automotive service, process payments, keep your service history, meet legal and regulatory requirements, and contact you about your vehicle. We do not use your information for third-party marketing.

Sharing

We share information only as needed to provide our services (for example, with our payment processor or as required by law). We never sell your personal information, and — as stated above — we never share your mobile number or SMS opt-in for anyone else's marketing.

Your choices

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. You may ask us to update or delete your contact information by contacting us.
